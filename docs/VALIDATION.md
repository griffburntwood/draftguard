# Validate the product with users

Status: planned; no user interviews, measured time savings, or expert sign-off are claimed.

Interview a shipping-document reviewer or freight-forwarding operator. Ask them to demonstrate their current SI/draft-BL checking and revision workflow using documents they are permitted to share. Observe rather than explain the intended answer.

Give the reviewer three synthetic tasks in DraftGuard: identify a mismatch and its evidence; decide what to do with a missing value; check a revised BL with one fixed and one newly introduced defect. Ask whether the outdated-review label matches their real approval process.

Record task completion, missed defects, time to locate supporting evidence, and points of confusion. Compare with their normal method on equivalent examples; do not claim an improvement from a single uncontrolled demonstration. Have a domain reviewer label a held-out document set before measuring accuracy and false acceptance.

Confirm these unresolved domain decisions: which source is authoritative when multiple SIs exist; whether notify-party wording may be equivalent; acceptable rounding/unit tolerances; document-release authority; required retention/access rules; and the expected category when a draft is requested but not attached.

The next product priority should follow those findings. Shared authenticated case storage and genuine mailbox integration need explicit requirements before the prototype can be used operationally.
